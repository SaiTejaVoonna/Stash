Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

In short

Stash has no accounts and no server. Everything you save stays on your device unless you choose to sync it to your own Google Drive. We don't collect, see, sell or share your data.

What Stash stores

Your notes, photos, files, collections and settings are stored in your browser's storage on your device. Vault items are encrypted with your password before they're stored.

What Stash never does

  • No analytics, tracking, ads or crash reports.
  • No account, sign-up or email.
  • No copy of your data on any server of ours.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, Stash signs in with Google on your device and asks only for access to files it creates (the drive.file permission). It writes your data to a “Stash” folder in your own Drive and reads it back to keep your devices in step. It can't see any other files in your Drive. Vault items are never synced. You can disconnect any time in Settings → Sync; the files stay in your Drive until you delete them. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account and Drive.

Features that download something once

Some optional features download a tool the first time you use them, then keep it on your device: the photo guesser (an image model), text from photos (a text reader), the PDF viewer, the QR reader and the zip reader. These are downloads only; your photos and files are never uploaded. You can switch features off in Settings → Add-ons.

Location and camera

Location is added to snaps and pins only if you allow it, and is stored only with that item on your device. The camera is used only while you take a photo, scan or read a QR code.

Your choices

Export everything from Settings → Your data at any time. Deleting a profile deletes its data from your device. Removing the site's data in your browser deletes everything Stash stored there.

Children

Stash isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the support email shown on Stash's Google sign-in screen.
